# Groove Link Jumia JForce + Temu affiliate catalogue

## Current storefront behaviour
- The affiliate discovery page is `marketplace.html`.
- It reads product records from `products.json`.
- Product cards open the original Jumia or Temu listing using the item's tracked affiliate URL.
- Customers complete checkout and payment on the marketplace, not on Groove Link.
- There is no 45% markup, Groove Link checkout, basket, or payment collection on this affiliate page.
- The catalogue is intentionally empty until the owner has approved programme access and real tracked product links.

## Product record format
Add products to the `products` array only when the affiliate link is your own valid tracking link and the product information/image use is permitted by the relevant programme.

```json
{
  "id": "unique-product-id",
  "name": "Accurate product title",
  "description": "Short factual description",
  "category": "Gadgets",
  "source": "Jumia",
  "affiliateUrl": "https://your-approved-tracked-link",
  "imageUrl": "",
  "priceGhs": 120,
  "approvedContent": true
}
```

Allowed source values: `Jumia` or `Temu`. Allowed categories: Fashion, Gadgets, Accessories, Jewellery, Home, Other.

- `affiliateUrl` must be the tracked link generated by the user's approved Jumia JForce or Temu affiliate account. Do not use an ordinary product URL and label it as an affiliate link.
- `imageUrl` is optional. Use only images the programme or rights holder permits you to display. If unsure, leave it blank; the card shows a placeholder.
- `priceGhs` is optional and should only be used for a clearly labelled approximate reference price. If omitted, the card says “See current price”. Marketplace prices can change; customers must check the live listing.
- Set `approvedContent` to true only after checking that the programme permits the product title, description and any image to be shown on Groove Link.

## Affiliate programme setup
1. Apply for Jumia Ghana's JForce programme: https://www.jumia.com.gh/mlp-jforce/
2. Apply for Temu's affiliate programme: https://www.temu.com/affiliate_recruit.html
3. Confirm that each programme accepts publishers based in Ghana, what payout methods/currency are available, minimum payout thresholds, and any content/link rules. Do not assume Temu payouts are available in Ghana until the application/dashboard confirms it.
4. Generate your own tracked links from each approved account.
5. Add only products and content permitted by the relevant programme terms.

## Important limitations and compliance
- This static GitHub Pages / Cloudflare Pages site does not scrape Jumia or Temu, sync stock or prices, create affiliate links, or accept payments.
- A Jumia JForce link and a Temu affiliate link must be generated inside their respective approved programme accounts. Groove Link cannot generate tracking links on your behalf.
- Do not scrape marketplace pages or copy/re-host product photos, descriptions, or other listing material unless the marketplace or rights holder has authorized that use. Jumia's Ghana buyer terms restrict republishing or commercially exploiting marketplace material except where expressly permitted: https://pay.jumia.com.gh/cms/customers-terms-and-conditions.html
- Jumia JForce is a commission/referral programme; it is not a wholesale feed or custom reseller checkout. Jumia handles fulfilment under its own terms.
- Temu affiliate rewards and eligibility are subject to the official local programme terms: https://www.temu.com/affiliate_question.html
- No API keys, access tokens, payment secrets or admin passwords belong in browser JavaScript or this repository.
- The existing original Groove Link collection on the home page remains a separate WhatsApp enquiry/reseller flow. Do not mix those products with affiliate products unless the selling and fulfilment model is clearly disclosed.
